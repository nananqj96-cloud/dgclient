# Creamy Keys recordings

All recordings in this bank are derived from physical recordings released under
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- Keyboard: "Mechanical keyboard typing sounds" by Reina0613 (2023), Ikki68
  Aurora with lubed WS Brown tactile switches, recorded with a Zoom H8.
  https://freesound.org/people/Reina0613/sounds/709460/
- Mouse clicks and wheel: "Mouse Clicks & Scrolls" by OwlStorm / Ashe Kirk
  (2015), Logitech mouse recorded with a Zoom H4n.
  https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/320146/
- Middle button: "Middle Mouse Click" by 1j01 (2026).
  https://opengameart.org/content/middle-mouse-click

Purxiy edits: isolated down/up pairs, gentle equalization, balanced gain and
smooth fades. Keyboard styles are tonal treatments of one keyboard recording.
The Freesound sources use the published high-quality MP3 previews; the middle
button uses original WAVs. No AI-generated audio, synthesized impacts or
artificial reverb. Source hashes and crop positions are in recordings.json.
